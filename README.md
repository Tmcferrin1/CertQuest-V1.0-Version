# CertQuest

**A free pixel RPG for studying the CompTIA A+ Core 2 (220-1202) exam.**

▶ **Play it in your browser:** https://tmcferrin1.github.io/CertQuest-.9-Version/

- Walk around a cozy pixel town, learn each exam objective from beginner-friendly lesson cards, and battle with real exam-style questions.
- 900+ questions, 38 hands-on PBQs, mock exams with a timer, typed command practice, spaced-repetition Daily Review, a Daily Challenge and an Endless Gauntlet.
- Catch 80 monsters: each one teaches an exam term.
- Works with a keyboard or a game controller. Your progress is saved in your browser.

CertQuest is a free study game. It is not affiliated with, sponsored by or endorsed by CompTIA. CompTIA and A+ are trademarks of CompTIA, Inc. Always check the official exam objectives on CompTIA's website.

The questions, lessons and monsters are original. Some map and character art comes from the free Ninja Adventure asset pack (CC0, public domain). Text font: DejaVu (see `LICENSE-DejaVu.txt`). Made with the Godot engine.
